# Pinecrest Outfitters — Store Policy

Pinecrest Outfitters is a fictional outdoor-gear store made for testing. This page is the published policy: the answer key is built from it.

## Shipping

- We ship to the United States and Canada only.
- Free standard shipping on US orders over $50. US orders under $50 pay $6.95 for standard shipping.
- US standard shipping takes 3–5 business days.
- US express shipping costs $15 and takes 1–2 business days.
- Shipping to Canada is a flat $12 and takes 5–10 business days. Canadian customers may owe import duties on delivery.
- Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern on a business day ship the same day.

## Returns

- You can return items within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, with the original tags attached.
- US returns are free: we email a prepaid return label.
- Canadian returns are accepted within the same 30 days, but the customer pays return shipping.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the return.
- There is no restocking fee.
- Items marked Final Sale cannot be returned.

## Warranty

- Tents, backpacks and jackets carry a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date.
- The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents or misuse.
- To make a claim, email support@pinecrest-outfitters.example with your order number and a photo of the defect.
- We repair or replace the item. If neither is possible, we refund the purchase price.

## Products

- **TrailLite 2 tent:** sleeps 2 people, weighs 1.8 kg (4 lb), 3000 mm waterproof rating, $249.
- **Summit 45 backpack:** 45 liters, weighs 1.4 kg, fits torso lengths of 16–21 inches, $179.
- **Ember down jacket:** 800-fill-power responsibly sourced (RDS) down, machine washable on a gentle cycle, $229.

## Subscriptions

- The Trail Box is a monthly subscription of trail snacks and small gear for $39 per month.
- Trail Box shipping is free in the US. Canadian subscribers pay $12 shipping per box.
- Cancel or skip a month anytime from your account page before the 25th of the month to stop the next box.
- There is no cancellation fee.

## Contact

- Email support@pinecrest-outfitters.example or use this chat.
- We do not offer phone support.
- Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm Eastern.
